The Homecoming

BIG WEEK has never been to Durban. But Durban has always been calling. In 2026, BIG WEEK answers that call.

This isn't a return to a city we've occupied before. It's a Homecoming to the music, culture, energy and people that feel naturally connected to what BIG WEEK represents.

We arrive with respect. Not to define Durban's culture, but to honour it, be shaped by it and place it on a grand stage.

Home is not a place.
It's a feeling.
