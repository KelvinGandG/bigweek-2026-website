FAQs

General

01How do I purchase a ticket?

Head over to bigweek.howler.co.za for the week Mzansi deserves.

Go to Howler
02Who can attend?

Anyone over the age of 18 can attend.

03Where can I enquire about table bookings?

Tables can be purchased directly through Howler, or you can enquire via tables@gandgpro.com.

Email tables@gandgpro.com
04When and where is BIG WEEK?

BIG WEEK Festival 2026 takes place on 27, 28, 29 and 30 December 2026. The venue will be announced before tickets go on sale on 5 October 2026.

05What time does the festival start?

Doors open at 15:00 and the festival closes at 00:00 (midnight).

06What can't I bring into the festival?

For everyone's safety, the following items are not allowed into the venue:

  • Firearms and weapons of any kind
  • Umbrellas
  • Camping chairs
  • Cooler boxes
  • Illegal substances
  • Your own alcohol
  • Outside food and beverages

We've got everything you need inside!

Right of admission reserved.

07Can I leave and re-enter the premises?

To ensure the safety and security of attendees, re-entry is not allowed during festival times.

08Will I be able to purchase food and beverages at the festival?

A wide variety of food and beverages will be available all day and night from the various vendors on the festival grounds.

Note: No food or beverages may be brought into the festival.

09Will parking be available at the venue?

There will be designated areas for hospitality guests and general parking.
